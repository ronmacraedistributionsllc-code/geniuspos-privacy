GeniusPOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

GeniusPOS is a point-of-sale app for small shops. It is built to keep your business data on your own devices. We do not run advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell data.

Data stored on your device

Products, stock, sales, customers, employees, settings and receipts are stored in the app on your device. This data stays on the device unless you turn on one of the optional features below, export a backup, or share a receipt.

Optional features that send data

Cloud Sync (optional): if you enter your own Supabase project and sign in, sales and customer records, and (with Online Ordering) your product catalog and online orders are stored in that project under your account so your devices can share them. The project belongs to you; the app's developer does not receive this data.

Online Ordering (optional): when you switch it on, your shop name, product names, prices and stock are published to your cloud project so customers can browse them. Customers who place an order give a name, phone number, optional email and a delivery address; that information goes to your cloud project and is visible to you.

WooCommerce and Loyverse (optional): if you connect them, product and stock information is exchanged with the store or account you specify.

Exchange rates (optional): when you ask the app to fetch a currency rate, it requests the public rate from open.er-api.com. No personal data is sent.

Email receipts: the app opens your own email app with a message already filled in. Nothing is sent until you send it.

Device permissions

Camera: used only to scan product barcodes. Images are not stored or uploaded.

Bluetooth: used only to connect to your receipt printer and label printer.

Photos: used only when you choose a picture for a product or your receipt logo.

Payments

GeniusPOS records the payment method and amount of each sale. It does not collect or store card numbers. If card payments are enabled in a future version, card data will be handled by the payment provider and not by GeniusPOS.

Deleting your data

You can delete your cloud account and all cloud data from Settings, under Cloud Sync, using Delete Cloud Account. Data on your device can be removed by deleting the app. Backups you exported are files that you control.

Children

GeniusPOS is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be shown in the app and on this page with a new date.
